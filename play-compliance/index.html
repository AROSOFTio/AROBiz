Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026

ARODesk is a business application providing inventory, sales, debtor tracking, invoices, and account/team management.

Data processed

We process account names and email addresses, business profile information, employee access roles, customer names, phone numbers and addresses, product details and photos, financial records and payment status, and documents entered into the app. Authentication credentials are managed by Supabase Auth.

How data is used and stored

Information is used to authenticate users, manage tenant access, store business records, produce invoices, and synchronize supported transactions. Cloud services are provided through Supabase. Some documents, preferences, and pending debtor repayments are stored locally on the device. The payment queue uses device secure storage and retries when an authenticated session and internet connection are available.

Camera and photos

Camera and gallery access is used only when a user chooses a product photo. Photos are resized before upload. Product photos are currently stored at publicly accessible image URLs: do not upload personal identification documents or confidential images as product photos. No background camera recording is performed.

Sharing and security

We use hosting and email providers for essential app functions. We do not sell personal data. Data sent to Supabase uses encrypted network connections. Tenant database access is controlled by authenticated roles and row-level security. Shared devices should be signed out after use.

Retention and deletion

Users may request account deletion from ARODesk Settings or using the form below. We verify ownership, review any businesses where the requester is the sole owner, then delete associated personal data. Financial records that must be retained for valid legal, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be retained with restricted access; we will explain any necessary retention during processing. A request is not an immediate deletion.

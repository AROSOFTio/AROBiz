Crown Business Docs — Privacy Policy

Version 2.2 · 24 November 2026

Crown Business Docs generates business documents directly on your device. No account or online service is required.

Local data

Business details, customer or supplier information and saved document records are stored in local app preferences. PDFs are generated on the device. By default these records stay on your device. If you create an optional account and explicitly select cloud backup, business details and document records are sent over HTTPS and stored encrypted on AROSOFT’s server. Backups are not end-to-end encrypted. Device operating system backups may apply.

Optional accounts and backups

We store your chosen username, a password hash, session information and any backup you explicitly upload. Backups are used to restore your records and can be deleted in Account & privacy. Password recovery is not yet available. You can delete your account and backup from the app; local documents remain on the device.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics are disabled by default. If enabled, the app sends a random installation identifier, event identifiers, app opens, document type, generation and save events, and share or print requests. Document content, names, contacts, amounts and PDFs are excluded from analytics. Reports are retained for up to 90 days. You can disable analytics and erase uploaded usage history in Account & privacy. Connection information is used temporarily for abuse prevention. No advertising profiles or sale of data.

Sharing and printing

When you choose Share or Print, your selected document is passed to the operating system and the app or printer you select. Their privacy practices apply. You control where copies go.

Deleting data

Delete individual documents from My documents. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, subject to device backups. Externally saved copies must be deleted separately. If you have an optional cloud account, use the account deletion option to remove its online records as well.

Legacy ARODesk accounts

The previous ARODesk service was a different product. This update does not migrate, erase or upload legacy records. For an existing ARODesk account or a legacy deletion request, contact arosoftio@gmail.com.

# ARODesk privacy and account deletion

**ARODesk** by **AROSOFT Innovations Ltd**. Updated 12 October 2026.

## Request account deletion

To request deletion of your ARODesk account and associated personal data without installing the app, email **[arosoftio@gmail.com](mailto:arosoftio@gmail.com?subject=ARODesk%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)** from your account email, or contact us to verify account ownership.

Please include **ARODesk account deletion** in the subject. Do not send passwords or financial details.

You may also submit a request within ARODesk under **Account & privacy → Request account deletion**. We verify requests and process account personal data deletion, while assessing the ownership of shared tenant records and legitimate accounting, financial, security and legal retention obligations. We will explain any required retention as part of handling your request.

## Privacy policy

ARODesk collects account names, email addresses, business information, employee access roles, customer names, phone numbers, addresses, transactions, invoices and product data to operate inventory, sales, documents and debtor tracking.

We use **Supabase** for authentication, databases and file hosting, and may use an email service to send account instructions. Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication.

**Camera / photos.** The app requests camera or gallery access only when you select a product image. Images are resized before upload. Product photos are currently available at public image URLs, so please do not upload confidential or identifying photographs.

**Device data.** Some standalone documents and preferences remain on the device. Pending debtor payments and cached debtor balances are stored using secure device storage and synchronized when the account reconnects; pending transactions remain unconfirmed until accepted by the server.

**Security.** We use authenticated access controls and tenant-based access policies for business data. Internet traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Users should sign out of shared devices.

**Sharing.** We do not sell personal data. Cloud, authentication and necessary email service providers process records to operate the app.

**Retention.** User account data may be removed on verified request, subject to any applicable retention of business accounting and financial records. We review requests involving a sole business owner before deletion.

**Privacy and deletion contact:** [arosoftio@gmail.com](mailto:arosoftio@gmail.com).
